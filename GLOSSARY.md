# open-dpp

open-dpp lets organizations create, publish and manage Digital Product Passports.

## Language

**Mail Link**:
The URL a mail gives its recipient for completing a flow: verifying an address, resetting a password, revoking an email change or accepting an invitation. While mail is off, the Mail Link goes to the server log instead of the recipient's inbox.
_Avoid_: action token, token, mail URL
