# Conformance Review: source list (public and gated)

Research note for the open-dpp Conformance Review (see `CONTEXT.md`, "Conformance Review"). Verified against primary sources on **2026-09-03**. Scope is fixed to Tier A (EU law) and Tier B (CEN/CENELEC JTC 24 harmonised standards); Tier C texts appear only where an A or B text invokes them normatively.

Local copies of gated texts live in the git-ignored `standards/` folder at the repo root. Never commit them.

## 1. Summary

- **Tier A is entirely public.** ESPR, the Batteries Regulation, the DPP registry Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1778 and the harmonised-standards Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1736 are on EUR-Lex. `eur-lex.europa.eu` blocks non-browser fetchers; the Publications Office Cellar endpoint serves the same text (see 2.).
- **Tier B is entirely gated.** All eight JTC 24 standards (EN 18216, 18219, 18220, 18221, 18222, 18223, 18239, 18246) are past enquiry. Six are published (2026-05-27) and cited in the OJ (2026-07-15); EN 18239 and EN 18246 are ratified with final text due 2026-09-16 and a second OJ citation planned for September 2026. No free enquiry draft remains on any national portal.
- The DPP standardisation request is **M/604** (C(2024) 5423, 31 July 2024) with amendment M/604 Amd 1, not M/598.
- The EU DPP registry went live on 2026-07-20; its API and semantic repository are defined in Reg. 2026/1778 Art. 3, 11, 12.
- Free secondary material of high value: the CEN-CENELEC DPP webinar slides (2026-06-25), the EC DPP FAQ (Dec 2025), CIRPASS-2 D4.1 reference architecture and its open-source test environment.
- **Tier C splits cleanly** (section 4, verified 2026-09-03): the ISO/IEC and IEC texts are gated (ISO/IEC 15459-1..6 CHF 379 at iso.org for the set, ISO/IEC 18975 CHF 100, IEC 61406-1/-2 EUR 27.28 each and IEC 63278-1 EUR 32.24 via EVS); everything else is free: GS1 Digital Link URI Syntax 1.7.0 and GS1-Conformant Resolver 1.2.1 (both Aug 2026), GS1 General Specifications 26.0, the IDTA AAS specifications Part 1 V3.2 and Part 2 V3.2.0 (Release 26-01, July 2026, CC-BY-4.0, with a DPP annex), ETSI EN 301 549 (V3.2.1 as cited, V4.1.1 of 2026-09 current) and ECMA-404 as the equivalent of ISO/IEC 21778. The ESPR-cited 2014/2015 editions of ISO/IEC 15459 are all still current.
- **The EU DPP registry API is not documented** (section 8): the SPA at `registry.product-passport.ec.europa.eu` calls `api.product-passport.ec.europa.eu/api/v1/…`, which only accepts an EU Login session cookie; no OpenAPI, developer portal or code repository exists, the only technical document is the Economic Operators user guide (v1.02, 2026-08-24, UI only). A test environment exists but needs a separate EU Login and a real QES/QSeal-verified organisation. Validation is in-app only (XML/JSON schema, UPI URL rules, 100 DPPs per file); the semantic validation service is "not yet available" and battery DPPs cannot be registered yet.
- **Tooling worth wrapping** (section 9): `gs1-syntax-engine` (npm `gs1encoder`) for GS1 Digital Link / AI syntax, `aas-test-engines` (`check_server`, `check_file`) for the AAS API and files, and the criteria of the GS1 resolver test suite ported to Playwright. The CIRPASS-2 "open source test environment" is an exploratory registry prototype (`mock-eu-registry` + `dpp-validator`), not a conformance suite. No JTC 24, national or Commission conformity-assessment scheme exists; the DPP service-provider delegated act is still planned.

## 3. Tier B — CEN/CENELEC JTC 24 harmonised standards

Committee: **CEN/CLC/JTC 24 "Digital Product Passport - Framework and System"**, secretariat DIN, established September 2023. Working groups: WG 1 Strategic Advisory Group, WG 2 Unique identifiers and data carriers, WG 3 Security, WG 4 Interoperability framework. The series answers standardisation request **M/604** (Commission Implementing Decision C(2024) 5423 final, 31 July 2024) and **M/604 Amd 1** (deadline moved to March 2026, batteries legal basis dropped, coverage statements added). There is no separate registry, data-processing or search standard: those topics sit inside EN 18216, EN 18222 and EN 18223; the registry itself is a Commission service.

### 9.5 Ranked: worth wrapping

1. **gs1-syntax-engine** (`gs1encoder`): maintained, Node-ready; assert every generated Digital Link URI and AI string in unit tests.
2. **aas-test-engines** `check_server` / `check_file`: official IDTA tooling with JSON output and exit codes; limited to 3.0.x read profiles and to endpoints open-dpp exposes.
3. **GS1DL-resolver-testsuite criteria** ported to Playwright against `/gs1/v1/01/{gtin}` (redirect, HEAD/OPTIONS, CORS, 400, query passthrough, `/.well-known/gs1resolver`, linkset), validating linksets with the `gs1/linkset` schema.
4. **@aas-core-works/aas-core3.x-typescript** `verification.verify()` on export payloads, in-process.
5. **CIRPASS-2 mock-eu-registry + dpp-validator** compose stack as a nightly exploratory integration test; not normative.
